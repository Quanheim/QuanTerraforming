# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-03

- Initial release.
- The sea's waves follow terrain edits (vanilla keeps the world-generated seabed). Server setting, on by default.
- Terrain limits: raise and dig beyond vanilla's 8 m (server settings, 200 m each by default). Replaces Heightmap
  Unlimited JvL, and stands aside if that mod is installed.
- The Terraforming Wand (2 Wood, no crafting station). Holding it shows the zone grid: zone borders, corner posts
  coloured by sea depth, and an on-screen box comparing the ground with what the sea is using.
